# Minimum fiscal evidence needed

No patient records are required. Use agreements, asset/funding records or an
aggregate reconciliation. Do not provide infant names, identifiers, dates of birth
or row-level journeys. Record source owner, version, applicable period, scope and
whether the document may be cited; keep source copies in the external evidence
vault, with a checksum and a non-confidential synthesis in the repository.

| Priority | Evidence | Minimum useful fields | Decision it resolves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retrieval charging schedule plus an aggregate invoice reconciliation | Financial period, referring HHS, mode, charged unit and aggregate quantity, asset/crew/escort/ground-leg inclusions, fixed availability, cancellation before/after tasking, actual charged amount and liable budget | Whether fewer clinically appropriate retrievals reduce CHHHS expenditure, and by how much |
| 2 | Programme/asset funding and hub agreement | Site/device owner, purchasing and replacement budget, grant restrictions, local staffing, pooled hub capacity, recharge basis | Which programme costs actually fall on CHHHS rather than another budget |
| 3 | Receiving and origin-care settlements | Aggregate destination/HHS routing, inter-HHS payment basis, incremental payment or cash release | Whether resource changes translate into district or state cash |
| 4 | Family and government funding rules | Applicable subsidy eligibility, reimbursement basis and marginal funding response | Consumer, state and Commonwealth scenarios |

Check contemporary terms for the historical model period separately from current
policy. Reconcile the resource components once to the health-system ledger;
then partition them by payer. Do not sum overlapping perspectives. Unknown
components remain unknown, with explicit scenario bounds rather than zeroes.

Preferred option: obtain priority 1 first while retaining the existing fixed,
shared and variable-payment scenarios. If only an agreement is available, it
can resolve liability without resolving avoided cash. If no records are
available, publish conditional fiscal scenarios and leave the evidence task open.
No request has been sent and no records have been manufactured.

## Policy interpretation and decision thresholds

Use fixed-wing asset liability after acceptance and tasking as current policy for the referring HHS. Self-sufficiency is a potential incentive interpretation; no behavioural response or policy objective is inferred. Keep stochastic exposure separate from that rule. In the six-policy CHHHS S7 schedule, interpolate each policy's cumulative net cash from fixed payments to full marginal retrieval cash. Comparing all pairwise intersections gives a direct switch from telephone to conditional OBSBOT expansion at 87.74% capture over three years and 68.58% over five. These are shares of modelled retrieval offset in dollars, not shares of trips or probabilities. The two choices tie at the crossing. Keep the economic resource rankings separate.

The internal CHHHS Inter Hospital Transfer procedure v9, effective 22 November 2024, pp. 1–3, corroborates distinct referral, acceptance, preparation and handover steps locally. Its scope is CHHHS; it supplies no statewide price or neonatal treatment effect. The local copy and checksum are under .local-evidence/triangulation. For registry design record consultation, acceptance, asset tasking, departure and eventual disposition separately, including referrals cancelled before and after tasking. For finance, seek aggregate mode-by-origin activity and charged units, costs retained under contract, and bundled versus separate escort and airport legs. Report attributable spend by referring HHS and resource effects once at state level. Test low and high activity against the observed device replacement year. Prioritise validating whether the dollar-weighted capture crosses the thresholds before refining a statewide average price.

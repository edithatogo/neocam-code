# Implementation decisions and remaining options

## Research decision

Use existing-pilot recruitment as the reference design. Wider-network enrolment
is an alternative only if study equipment/access can be supplied before recruitment;
its enabling cost is unpriced. A five-year planning horizon and equal attenuated/null
scenario weights are explicit assumptions. Initial, eventual and rescue endpoints
are separate design alternatives. No observational scenario identifies a causal effect.

Options for the team are to retain the pilot, fund a comparative registry, or make
a conditional expansion decision now. Use perspective-specific net value and its
Monte Carlo uncertainty, then price local collection and enabling costs before
committing funds. Do not select a design solely from a modelled positive mean.
The CHHHS cash analysis and health-system resource analysis answer different questions.

## Billing

Recommended next evidence step: obtain the applicable charging schedule and an
aggregate invoice-to-task reconciliation. Fixed-wing referring-HHS liability is
already evidenced. The remaining question is the marginal bill, including mode,
cancellation and settlement rules. Scenario analysis remains the interim option;
zero/partial/full cash capture must not be presented as observed contracts.
This is the still-open fiscal track, not an implementation blocker to reporting
explicit scenarios.

## Perspective availability

Mixed policies: all four portfolios have health-system and bounded societal
resource ledgers. The existing CHHHS reference financing scenario additionally
supports origin-site, district, state, consumer and Commonwealth annual ledgers;
its source documents which allocations are assumptions. It is not extended to
other HHSs without support. Uniform comparisons use paired archives where retained;
expected loss and EVPI are unavailable from marginal summaries alone.

Joint-model registry designs use the two resource perspectives. The sequential
CHHHS cash example keeps the earlier known-baseline signal and point programme
costs, compares billing before/after clarification on a common five-year horizon,
and labels that narrower estimand. It is not interchangeable with joint resource
EVSI. Other registry fiscal perspectives are not estimated by silently relabelling
a resource ledger.

## Geography and publication

Use the attributable public facility points and Natural Earth boundary. Yarrabah
remains labelled without an invented point. The map ledger binds every assignment
and eligibility status; the private supplement supplies a facility key. Network
information values are never allocated as site savings. Six main-article displays
are preserved; new figures belong in the supplement or graphical abstract.

The Word edit in the article checkout remains uncommitted and untouched. Patient-
derived publication candidates stay in the external evidence vault. Publication,
secondary-use/disclosure determinations, local cost quotes, recruitment feasibility
and four independent reviewer returns remain separate from implementation acceptance.

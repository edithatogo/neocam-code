# Aggregate neonatal consultation and retrieval extract

Request the latest three complete years, annually by referring HHS. If permitted and counts allow, separate pilot sites and calendar periods before/after video availability. Supply covered facilities, inclusion criteria, missing periods, and changes in service or coding. Exclude antenatal maternal transfers, scheduled repatriations and repeat contacts from the urgent postnatal infant episode count, or identify them separately.

Preferred denominators and outcomes: eligible distinct infant episodes, consultation episodes (telephone only, any video, unknown), immediate transfer decision, initially observed locally, subsequently transferred within 24 hours of first specialist advice, subsequently transferred later, completed locally and unknown follow-up. For the immediate-transfer group report completed transport within 24 hours separately where feasible. Record how long follow-up is available. Flag missing consultation times. Use broad gestation or acuity bands only if disclosure controls permit.

If only retrieval records are available: unique infants, accepted referrals, tasked journeys, completed transports and cancellations before/after tasking by referring HHS, year and mode. Separate legs, journeys and infants; retain mixed-mode or unknown categories. Indicate whether dates refer to referral, tasking, departure or arrival. A retrieval-only extract can inform workload and mode mix but lacks infants observed locally.

Use the approved aggregate release process and small-cell suppression. No names, identifiers, exact dates, patient-level records or free text. Suppressed, unknown and zero counts must be distinguishable.
